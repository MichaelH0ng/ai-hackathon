01 / Real problem, real userNorthStar

Built for a student balancing today's spending with a goal.

WHO WE'RE DESIGNING FOR A student or first-time budgeter saving toward something specific.

They need to make everyday choices without losing sight of a near-term goal.

THE MOMENT THAT MATTERS

“What will this purchase change for me?”

A price tag gives the cost today. NorthStar makes the possible trade-off visible in hours of work and time to a goal.

Validation gap — be transparent

03 · EXPLAIN

Keep the decision human

NorthStar shows the time impact. The person decides whether that trade-off is worth it.

Reliable arithmetic

Goal dates and purchase delays come from app formulas—not generated model math. Assumptions are shown beside the result.

Clear fallback

When Polaris AI is unavailable, common questions can be answered on-device. AI requests send relevant context to the configured provider.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. Photo scanning needs the configured AI service; typed prices work without it.
